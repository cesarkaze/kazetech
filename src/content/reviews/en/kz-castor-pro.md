In-ear monitors (IEMs) have become the preferred choice for many competitive gamers and creators looking to eliminate the weight of a traditional headband without losing audio fidelity. The **KZ Castor** stands out in the entry-level range thanks to its dual-driver configuration.

### Sound Signature and Soundstage

The instrumental separation is clean, allowing you to identify footsteps and spatial audio cues with precision in multiplayer matches. The base equalization curve offers impactful bass without muddying the mids where vocals reside.

### Physical Tuning Switches

A standout feature of this model is the inclusion of adjustable micro-switches directly on the shell. They allow you to modulate the bass response and treble clarity directly via hardware without relying on third-party software.
