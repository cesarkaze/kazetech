The **AULA F75** has become a standout option in the budget mechanical keyboard space by offering features typically associated with more expensive enthusiast boards. While premium keyboards can easily climb past the $150 mark, the **F75** focuses on delivering an appealing typing experience, extensive connectivity, and a customizable design at a much more accessible price. It's particularly attractive to anyone looking to upgrade their desk without sacrificing style or functionality.

### Competitive Advantage: Gasket-Mounted Design & Customization

One of the **F75**'s biggest selling points is its **gasket-mounted** construction, paired with internal sound-dampening materials designed to soften keystrokes and reduce unwanted resonance. Combined with compatible **factory-lubed linear switches**, the keyboard can deliver the smooth, rounded sound profile often described as creamy or thocky. The exact result depends on the switch variant, typing technique, and desk surface, but its **out-of-the-box typing** **experience** is a major part of the appeal. 

For gaming, the **F75** offers responsive input performance suitable for a wide range of titles, while its comfortable layout also works well for writing, studying, and everyday productivity. However, users who regularly work with spreadsheets may miss a dedicated numeric keypad, and the configuration software is less sophisticated than enthusiast-oriented alternatives such as **QMK/VIA-compatible keyboards**.

#### Where It Falls Short

Despite its premium-inspired typing experience, the **F75** doesn't deliver premium materials across the board. **Its ABS plastic** case feels less substantial than the aluminum chassis found on more expensive keyboards. Some users may also find the typing feel too flexible or the sound too noticeable for a quiet workspace. **Software limitations** and differences between switch and keycap variants are worth considering before purchasing.

## KazeTech VERDICT

The **AULA F75** is an excellent entry point into mechanical keyboards for anyone who wants a stylish, customizable, and versatile upgrade without spending a fortune. Its gasket-mounted design, hot-swappable switches, tri-mode connectivity, and satisfying typing feel make it a compelling choice for gaming and everyday productivity. It isn't a flawless enthusiast keyboard, but its combination of features and affordability is difficult to overlook. If you're building a clean, personalized setup and want more than the basics, the **F75** deserves a place on your shortlist.
