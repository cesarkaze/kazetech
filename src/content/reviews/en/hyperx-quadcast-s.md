The **HyperX QuadCast S** has remained a standard on the desks of content creators and streamers. Its greatest appeal lies in the convenience of having an all-in-one solution without the need for external audio interfaces or bulky pop filters.

### Acoustic Performance

Equipped with three 14mm condenser capsules, it offers four selectable polar patterns: stereo, omnidirectional, cardioid, and bidirectional. For the vast majority of creators, cardioid mode is the right choice, effectively isolating mechanical keyboard typing in the background.

### Connectivity and Physical Control

The USB-C connector on the back ensures direct compatibility with PC, consoles, and Mac. The gain dial at the bottom base allows for real-time input volume adjustments without having to open control panels in Windows.
