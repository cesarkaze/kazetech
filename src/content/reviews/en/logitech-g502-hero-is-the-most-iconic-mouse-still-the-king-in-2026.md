The **Logitech G502 HERO** is probably the best-selling and most recognizable gaming mouse on the market. Although current trends favor ultra-lightweight mice, this peripheral remains the undisputed favorite for those seeking versatility, extra buttons, and **unmatched ergonomics** for medium to large hands.

In the lab tests, the **25,600 DPI HERO optical sensor** performed flawlessly: zero hardware acceleration, smoothing, or filtering, ensuring your crosshair in games like **_Counter-Strike 2_** or **_Valorant_ goes exactly where your hand intends.

Furthermore, if you work in **video editing** or **design**, or if you **play MMOs**, its 11 programmable buttons and hyper-fast scroll wheel are absolute game-changers. It is a true workhorse mouse that justifies every single penny.
